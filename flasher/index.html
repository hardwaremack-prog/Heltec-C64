Back up or clone a board

Save everything on a board (firmware, settings, ROMs, cartridge, disks) to one file, or put a saved file back on the same board or onto another Heltec V3. Works with any ESP32 board, so you can also back up Meshtastic before installing the C64.

Reads the whole flash (8 MB, about 2 minutes) and saves a .bin file.

How to flash

  1. Plug the Heltec V3 into this computer with a USB-C cable that carries data (some charge-only cables don't).
  2. Click INSTALL HELTEC-C64, pick the board's port in the list (usually CP210x or USB Serial) and click Connect.
  3. Choose whether to erase: tick Erase the first time (coming from Meshtastic). When updating, leave it unticked so your uploaded ROMs and disk files are kept.
  4. Click Install and wait about a minute until it says done. Then press the board's RST button.
  5. The OLED shows the next step: join the C64-Setup Wi‑Fi (password commodore64) and open http://192.168.4.1/settings to put it on your network.

If it doesn't connect

  • No port in the list on Windows? Install the CP210x USB driver, unplug and replug the board.
  • Still stuck? Hold the PRG button, tap RST, let go of PRG, then click Install again.
  • Close anything else using the board's port first (Arduino IDE, Meshtastic app, serial monitors).

About backups and clones

  • A backup is an exact copy of the board's flash. Restoring it puts the board back exactly as it was, including its Wi‑Fi settings.
  • Cloning a C64 board gives the copy your ROMs, cartridge, disk images and Wi‑Fi settings, ready to play.
  • Cloning a Meshtastic board also copies its private encryption keys, so two radios would share one identity. Restore a Meshtastic backup onto the same board, or factory-reset the copy in the Meshtastic app afterwards.
  • Backups contain your Wi‑Fi password and anything you uploaded. Keep them private, and don't publish them on GitHub.

Going back to Meshtastic

Restore your Meshtastic backup with Restore / clone, or flash a fresh copy from flasher.meshtastic.org.
